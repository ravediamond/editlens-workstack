- `~/venvs/editlens` (Python 3.10, `--system-site-packages`, JetPack torch 2.5.0a0 nv24.08 reused untouched): transformers 4.46.3, peft 0.17.1, accelerate 1.1.1, tokenizers 0.20.3, safetensors 0.8.0, emoji 2.16.0 (`requirements-editlens.txt`). **Differs from the prompt's plan:** transformers 5.20.2 (the latest) does not import on torch 2.5 (`NameError: nn` in `integrations/accelerate.py`), and peft 0.13.2 cannot read the adapter's config (`alora_invocation_tokens`, written by peft 0.18.1); peft 0.17.1 reads it and ignores four newer fields (`alora_invocation_tokens`, `arrow_config`, `ensure_weight_tying`, `peft_version`). Model loaded and scored; the adapter's score head is checked against the saved tensors on every run.
- fp16 on the GPU, no quantization, as the prompt says. **Not what the official script does:** `scripts/inference.py:125` loads the base in 4-bit nf4 (the adapter was trained as QLoRA); an fp16 base differs slightly from that, so these scores are close to, not identical with, the official ones.

## What the EditLens code says (pangramlabs/EditLens, read 2026-11-04; the model card has no usage example)
- Score decoding, `scripts/inference.py:199-203`: `probs = softmax(logits)`; `score = (probs @ arange(n_buckets)) / (n_buckets - 1)`; this adapter has 4 buckets (score head shape (4, 3072); `configs/llama.yaml:7`), so score = (p1 + 2 p2 + 3 p3) / 3.
- Input, `scripts/inference.py:178-179` and `scripts/preprocess.py:42-48`: `clean_text` (demojize, drop a `</think>` prefix, drop a first paragraph starting with Sure / Here / Abstract / Title / Certainly, **lower-case**, collapse whitespace), then truncation at `max_length` 1024. Head: `NormedLinear` (LayerNorm + bias-free linear), `scripts/train.py:111-120`, put in place of the base model's `score` layer before the adapter is loaded (`scripts/inference.py:141`).
- Thresholds: **none are published** in the repo or the card. `configs/llama.yaml:8-9` has `lo_threshold: 0.03`, `hi_threshold: 0.15`, but those turn a text-distance score into the 4 training buckets; they are not detection thresholds. The ternary labels come from two thresholds calibrated on a validation set by F1 (`scripts/eval/ternary_eval.py:45-62`, `scripts/eval/threshold.py`); their values are not in the files. So the map has **no human / AI labels**, only raw scores next to the references below.
- Training used texts of at least 75 words (`configs/llama.yaml:11`); sections under 40 words are marked short and noisy.

## Limits
Pangram's open 3B is weaker than the production detector (their own numbers: ternary macro-F1 0.895 against 0.920; RAID binary 0.930 against 0.992), the references are three papers, the thresholds are unknown, and the PDF text is extracted in reading order with figures filtered by a heuristic. A low score here predicts little about alphaXiv; a high one is a pointer to a paragraph to read again.

## Addendum 2026-11-04 (later): rendered text, and what changed in the first reading
`scripts/paper_map.py` now scores the rendered markdown (`paper/DRAFT_4p.md`, numbers filled in) when it exists, because extraction turns `{{placeholders}}` in the source into "N". I expected the blanked numbers to inflate the scores; they did not (rendered means are 0.01 to 0.03 higher).
