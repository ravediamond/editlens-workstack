  (`MY_STYLE.md`). K and L: what the open EditLens model reacted to in this author's four papers.
- `EDITLENS_LEXICON.md`: words that go with higher and lower sentence scores (weak: a lexicon from three papers predicts the fourth at r 0.116).
- `REWRITE_NOTES.md`: what the highest-scoring paragraphs of the first map had in common.
