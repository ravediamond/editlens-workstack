# Rules

- `WRITING_RULES.md`: 92 rules in groups A to L. A to J: writing a short experimental paper that a reader can check, in the author's voice
  (`MY_STYLE.md`). K and L: what the open EditLens model reacted to in this author's four papers, kept as findings about the detector.
  They are not a target: the owner stopped optimising against the score on 2026-11-06 (see `../docs/WORKFLOW.md` §4).
- `EDITLENS_LEXICON.md`: words that go with higher and lower sentence scores (weak: a lexicon from three papers predicts the fourth at r 0.116).
- `REWRITE_NOTES.md`: what the highest-scoring paragraphs of the first map had in common.
- Rule 40 is the one that governs the rest: the score is a pointer to paragraphs to reread, never a measure of quality and never evidence of authorship.
- Rules 13, 60, 61, 66 and 67 were deleted on 2026-11-05 after controlled edits showed they raised the score; they are kept struck through.
